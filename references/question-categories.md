# Question Categories & Seeds (role-agnostic)

Use the categories as tags in the question bank. Role-specific seeds live in `role-packs/`.

## Categories (tags)
`intro` · `motivation` · `screening` · `behavioural` · `leadership` · `conflict` · `failure` · `ambiguity` · `prioritisation` · `role-craft` (case, product sense, system design, portfolio…) · `technical` · `strategy` · `culture-fit` · `exec` · `reverse` (questions to ask them) · `real-interview` (asked in a real interview)

## By round

### Recruiter screen
- Walk me through your background.
- Why are you looking to move now?
- Why this company / this role?
- What are you looking for in your next role?
- Location, work arrangement, notice period, expectations.
- What do you know about our product?

### Hiring manager
- Tell me about something you owned end-to-end.
- How do you decide what to work on next?
- A time you disagreed with a peer or your manager.
- How do you work with the teams around you day to day?
- What would your first 90 days look like?

### Role-craft / case / technical
See the role pack.

### Culture fit
- How do you like to work? What environments do you thrive in?
- A team you loved, and why.
- How do you handle feedback you disagree with?
- What would your last team say is hard about working with you?

### Exec / final
- Where is this market going, and where should we play?
- What's the biggest risk to our strategy?
- Tell me about influencing an org-level decision.

### Reverse (always prepare 3–5)
- What does great look like in this role after 6 months?
- What's the hardest unsolved problem on the team right now?
- How are decisions made between <team A> and <team B>?
- What made the last person in this role successful (or not)?
