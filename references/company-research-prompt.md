# Company & Interviewer Research Prompt (Mode 2)

If web research is available, run this directly. Otherwise give it to the user to run in a deep-research tool. Fill in the brackets.

```
Research [COMPANY] for a [ROUND TYPE] interview for the role of [ROLE]. Interviewers: [NAMES + TITLES + any profile notes].

1. Company: origin, business model, revenue scale, funding/ownership (incl. any acquisition, JV, or parent company), key leaders and what they say publicly.
2. Product: core products, recent launches (last 12–18 months), roadmap signals, pricing/positioning.
3. Market: top competitors, how [COMPANY] differentiates, where it's winning/losing, analyst views.
4. Strategy: stated priorities, recent strategic moves, risks and open questions.
5. Culture: values in their own words, how teams work, engineering/product culture signals, employee reviews (weigh carefully), post-change dynamics (acquisition, reorg, layoffs).
6. Interviewers: background, career path, public talks/posts, what they care about, what that suggests about what they'll probe.
7. The role: what problems this team likely owns, how the JD's requirements map to the business.

Output: clear sections, sources for every claim, flagged gaps or contradictions, and a closing list of
(a) 5 things to weave into answers naturally, (b) 5 smart questions to ask these specific interviewers.
```

Save findings into the target brief, not the profile.
